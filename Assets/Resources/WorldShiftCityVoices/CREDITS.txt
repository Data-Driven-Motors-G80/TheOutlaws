City street conversations

Original dialogue and stereo crowd mix created for The Outlaws.
Speech synthesized locally with Windows desktop text-to-speech; no recorded actors or downloaded samples.
Generator: Tools/Audio/generate_city_voices.ps1 and Tools/Audio/mix_city_crowd.cjs.
Twenty short phrases are layered into WS_CityCrowd.wav; individual phrases play intermittently from each footpath.
The generator uses David/Zira when available, with David as the fallback and varied prosody/rates.
